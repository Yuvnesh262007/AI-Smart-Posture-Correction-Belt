POSTURE BELT FINAL CAD — V4

Design goal: discreet upper-back wearable using measured components.

PRINTABLE PARTS
- main_base_final.stl / .step
- main_lid_final.stl / .step
- battery_cradle_final.stl / .step
- battery_lid_final.stl / .step
- motor_mount_final.stl / .step (print 3)

DESIGN
- Main pod outer envelope: 68 x 82 x 21 mm nominal.
- Battery pod outer envelope: 52 x 36 x 14.5 mm nominal.
- Motor mount: 22 x 18 x 6.5 mm; motor pocket approx 9.2 mm diameter.
- Main enclosure uses full perimeter edge fillets on both outer/front and body-facing/rear edges.
- Rounded exterior is intended to reduce snagging and pressure points.
- Main and battery enclosures are serviceable two-piece assemblies.

COMPONENT FIT
ESP32: 57 x 30 x 15 mm measured.
Battery: 45 x 27 x 9–10 mm measured.
TP4056: 31 x 19 x 4–5 mm measured.
MPU6050: 20 x 16 x 3–4 mm measured.
Slide switch: 15 x 10 x 17–18 mm measured.
Motor: 8 x 4 mm measured.

PRINTING
PETG recommended. Start with 0.2 mm layer height, 3–4 walls, 20–30% infill.
Do not print all three motor mounts unless needed; print 3 for the final harness.

IMPORTANT
This is the final CAD review package, but do a low-cost fit/test print before ordering a production-quality print.
The USB-C cutout and switch opening should be test-fitted against the actual hardware.
Do not rely on the CAD alone for battery safety; the battery must be protected from puncture, compression and heat.
